# Editorial Overview

> A medium-length framing for visitors who want more than the README hero
> and less than the full manifesto.

## The frame

ZéNí is a venture concerned with the **operational layer above AI models**. Most AI commentary in 2026 still treats the model as the product. ZéNí does not. We treat the model as a substrate that is rapidly commoditizing, and we treat the coordination layer above it — discovery, routing, trust, evidence, governance — as the next platform.

That framing is not unique to us. The framing is the entry condition for working seriously in this space at all. What is distinctive about ZéNí is the willingness to commit, structurally and from the beginning, to building that operational layer rather than retrofitting it onto a model-first product later.

## The editorial posture

This repository is the venture's public voice. Its job is to make the conceptual ground covered by ZéNí legible — not just to investors or developers, but to anyone trying to think clearly about where AI is going.

We hold ourselves to four editorial commitments:

- **Confident without overclaiming.** Where we say "we believe," we mean it as a position the reader can disagree with, not a marketing line.
- **Substantive without being academic.** Every concept is introduced through its consequences in real work, not through theory for its own sake.
- **Plainspoken without being thin.** Short prose where short prose is honest; long prose where the argument earns it.
- **Educational, not promotional.** The reader should leave with a sharper mental model regardless of whether they ever engage with the venture.

The thesis of the venture is itself the most useful artifact we can publish. If the thesis is right, the implementation is interesting. If the thesis is wrong, no amount of implementation polish will save it. We publish the thesis openly because we think it is right and because the conversation is more interesting when it can be had in public.

## Where the editorial sits in the repository

The pieces in this repository serve different reading distances:

- The **manifesto** is the venture's worldview, in long form. Read it when you want to know what ZéNí believes.
- The **explainers** (`AGENTIC_AI.md`, `AGENTIC_WEB.md`, `WHY_MCP_MATTERS.md`) are the conceptual ground, in essay form. Read them when you want to understand the field.
- The **positioning piece** (`WHY_ZENI.md`) is the timing argument. Read it when you want to know why this matters now.
- The **primers** in [`docs/primers/`](../primers/) are the same conceptual ground at a shorter reading distance. Read them when you have ten minutes, not forty.
- The **glossary and FAQ** are reference surfaces. Use them as you would any reference.

You do not need to read everything. Pick the reading distance that fits your time and curiosity, and trust that any one entry point will get you most of the way to the thesis.
