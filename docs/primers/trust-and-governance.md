# Trust and Governance in Agentic Systems

Agentic systems need trust because they can act across tools and workflows.

Trust does not mean believing the system is always right.

Trust means the system gives people enough structure to understand, review, approve, and improve what happened.

Governance matters because agentic work creates new questions:

- what can the system access?
- who approved the action?
- what tools were used?
- what evidence was created?
- what happened if something failed?
- how can a human intervene?

ZeNi treats governance and evidence as part of the product experience, not as afterthoughts.
