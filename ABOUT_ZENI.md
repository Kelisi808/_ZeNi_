# About ZéNí

## In one paragraph

ZéNí is building governed coordination for autonomous agent teams. It connects
PitStop, the human command surface, with SideQuest, the machine routing and
accountability layer. Together they turn ambiguous intent into inspectable
execution where permissions, routing, outcomes, and evidence remain visible.

## The missing operating layer

Models are becoming capable enough to do meaningful work. The operating layer
around that work is still fragile.

A model can answer a prompt. A tool can perform an action. A real mission raises
harder questions: who may act, which capability should receive the work, what
limits apply, when a person must intervene, what outcome was actually produced,
and what evidence remains afterward.

ZéNí treats those questions as infrastructure.

## One system, three surfaces

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| **PitStop** | The operator cockpit for mission intake, visibility, intervention, artifact review, evaluation, and export. |
| **SideQuest** | The machine-facing layer for capability discovery, routing, temporary skill access, task state, outcomes, receipts, and performance evidence. |
| **ZéNí** | The trust fabric that keeps governance, evidence standards, readiness, continuity, and learning boundaries coherent across the loop. |

PitStop faces the human. SideQuest faces the machine. ZéNí holds the operating
doctrine between them.

## The operating loop

ZéNí is not another chatbot or a single-agent framework. It is the loop that
makes autonomous work visible enough to trust:

1. **Describe** the mission, constraints, and desired outcome.
2. **Route** agents, models, skills, tools, and policy paths.
3. **Execute** with state and boundaries visible while work is happening.
4. **Review** artifacts, outcomes, receipts, and exceptions through a human
   command surface.
5. **Learn** from completed work through controlled comparison, without turning
   a weak signal into silent policy.

The moat is the governed loop, not a dashboard or an audit log in isolation.

## Current status

ZéNí is in **live pilot readiness**. PitStop, SideQuest, and the ZéNí trust
surface are deployed and connected, but the system is not generally available.
The current focus is structured use with startup operators: first-run clarity,
mission success, artifact quality, revision recovery, truthful status, and
evidence that helps a person decide what to do next.

The public proof boundary is deliberately narrow:

- [PitStop](https://ps-cockpit-jtbcd9.fly.dev/) shows the operator surface.
- [SideQuest](https://sq-route-jtbcd9.fly.dev/) shows the machine-facing surface.
- [ZéNí](https://zn-hub-jtbcd9.fly.dev/) shows the public trust surface.
- [The system said no](CAUSAL_OPERATING_SYSTEM_PROOF.md) documents one bounded
  controlled-replay result and the decision not to promote a weak lesson.

These surfaces are product and category proof, not a claim of market validation
or unrestricted autonomous authority.
