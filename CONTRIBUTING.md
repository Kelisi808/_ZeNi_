# Contributing

This repository is the public editorial home for ZéNí. It is curated, not crowdsourced. Most of what lives here is hand-written by the founder or by AI assistants working under the editorial conventions documented in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). That said, thoughtful contributions are welcome where they sharpen the public material rather than expand it.

## What an excellent contribution looks like

The kind of contribution worth opening an issue or pull request for:

- **A clarification.** A sentence in an essay that reads ambiguously, a glossary entry that overloads a term, or a primer that buries the central point. If you can make the existing prose land more cleanly, that is genuinely valuable.
- **A factual correction.** Particularly in [`WHY_MCP_MATTERS.md`](WHY_MCP_MATTERS.md), [`AGENTIC_AI.md`](AGENTIC_AI.md), or anywhere that touches the broader industry. If the field has moved or we got something wrong, we want to know.
- **A glossary refinement.** Definitions that are too narrow, too broad, or out of step with how a term is actually used in 2026.
- **A translation polish.** The hand-written summaries in [`Language Options/`](Language%20Options/) cover six languages. Native speakers will sometimes catch phrasings that read awkwardly. Suggestions are welcome.
- **An editorial expansion.** If a primer or essay would clearly benefit from one more section, and you can write it in the existing voice, propose it. The bar is high but not closed.

## What is out of scope

This is a public editorial repository, not a product repository. The following do not belong here, regardless of how well-written they are:

- Product implementation details — source code, partial code fragments, internal API contracts, schemas.
- Architecture descriptions that go below the conceptual level documented in the existing essays.
- Proprietary routing logic, model allocation specifics, or any mechanism that would reveal internal pipeline mechanics.
- Internal product roadmaps with dates or commitments.
- Strategy memos or internal system documentation.
- Code from `Kelisi808/ZeNi`, `Kelisi808/pitstop`, or `Kelisi808/sidequest`.

If a contribution would turn this public context layer into internal system documentation, it does not belong here — even if the contribution is otherwise good. The relevant test: "would a competitor learn something useful from this that they could not have inferred from a published essay or industry article?" If yes, the content belongs in a product working tree.

## Style

New prose should match the tone of the existing essays:

- Declarative sentences. The reader trusts a position more when the writer trusts it themselves.
- Plain words for big ideas. "Operable intelligence" earns its weight; "synergistic AI paradigm" does not.
- Argument structure that the reader can disagree with. We are publishing a thesis, not a creed.
- Short paragraphs where they are honest; long paragraphs where the argument earns the length.

For the visual artifacts (SVG diagrams, the public layer diagram), the visual grammar is mineral charcoal palette, restraint, and editorial typography. Use the existing diagram as a reference.

## How to contribute

For now, contributions are by issue or pull request through GitHub. There is no separate contributor program, no CLA, and no expectation of ongoing involvement — a one-time clarification is welcome.

If you are unsure whether something belongs, open an issue first and ask. We would rather have the conversation than reject a thoughtful PR after the fact.

For larger ideas — a new primer, a new language summary, a substantial editorial direction — the founder's [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kelisi/) is the most reliable channel before opening work.
